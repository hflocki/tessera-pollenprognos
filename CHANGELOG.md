# Changelog

## 0.5.0
- A tap on the tile opens a card with every allergen and three days.
- New top bar item **Strong pollen**: "Birch: high" while an allergen reaches the level you set. Two new settings on
  the screen: whether the top bar says it, and from which level (5, moderate to high, at first).

## 0.4.0
- New in the inspector: **Look**: circles with words, circles only, or bars. The bars of 0.2 are back as a choice
  and take less room. **Level in words** is now part of it.

## 0.3.0
- Circles as in the pollenprognos card: per day a ring that fills from none to high in green, amber or red, with
  the allergen's icon in the middle; they grow with the tile, three allergens fill a tile two cells high.
- New in the inspector: **Level in words** under the circles.

## 0.2.0
- A new look: a bar per day that fills from none to high in green, amber or red, the level in words, the icon in
  the colour of today, and the allergens spread over the whole tile.
- With no pollen in the air the tile shows a green flower and says so.

## 0.1.0
- First version: the pollen levels of the DWD Pollenflug sensors you choose, today and up to two days ahead.
